General Bayesian inference + SDE parameter tuning This will be a living document where I add the distributions, functions, and properties that I find most helpful and critical when working on problems related to Bayesian statistics. Bayesian statistics is a fascinating but, in my opinion, challenging field. In terms of approach, it feels much more open-ended compared to Frequentist, making it overwhelming when deciding how to start a problem. Formulations of posteriors can require some tricks and tools that are not rigorously covered in fundamental math courses (Single/Multivariable Calculus, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations). AI tools have no problem with implementation. This document will focus on the pen and paper side of problems, so that the reader may build their fundamental understanding of the field and leave the grunt work of translation to the agents.

Gamma Function

The Gamma function is defined as: $$ \Gamma(n) = \int_0^\infty x^{n-1}e^{-x}\,dx $$ For positive integers, \( \Gamma(n) = (n-1)! \) [Wikipedia].
